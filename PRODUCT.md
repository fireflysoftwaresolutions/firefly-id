# Firefly Identity

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

A Firefly-branded distribution of Keycloak. Existing Keycloak capabilities and upstream behavior remain the technical foundation; theme resources provide the Firefly identity. Build and packaging details belong to the repository's implementation documentation.

## Users

People signing in to customer-facing applications, and operators administering their identity service. The customer identity and access management scope comes from the authorized product brief; no customer deployments or acceptance results are asserted here.

## Product Purpose

Provide a coherent Firefly identity experience around Keycloak, under the product name **Firefly Identity**.

## Operating Context

Authentication, account management and identity administration are operational tasks. They must remain understandable, keyboard accessible and compatible with the actual supported upstream themes and flows.

## Brand Commitments

- The visible product name is Firefly Identity; technical repository and theme slugs remain `firefly-id`. Visual wordmarks use lowercase `firefly identity`.
- Belong to the same visual family as actual firefly koinon and liquidOS: canonical Firefly colors, Manrope, precise geometry and restrained amber.
- Preserve the official parent Firefly wordmark; do not retype or redraw it.
- The product identity must communicate protected identity immediately. Meaningful abstraction is welcome: a structural arch and geometric cradle protect a central amber identity. Preserve legibility at 16–24 px and avoid letter puzzles or generic keyholes.
- Use Manrope throughout every layout, form and interface, as well as brand lettering.
- The repository banner states the CIAM purpose directly; avoid generic experience slogans.
- Use original editable vector artwork for the product identity. The private brand kit is reference material, not a redistributable dependency.
- Repository-facing copy is English. Trademark rights remain separate from software licensing.

## Evidence on Hand

`assets/brand/provenance.json` records verified current source revisions and the precise files used from the private brand kit, koinon and the website containing liquidOS. `assets/brand/index.html` is the visual reference for the original Firefly Identity identity.

## Product Principles

- Keep identity tasks clear and focused.
- Preserve upstream behavior while changing presentation.
- Use the established Firefly family consistently.
- State validation and deployment boundaries accurately.
